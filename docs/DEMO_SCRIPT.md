# Demo Script (3–5 minutes)

1. Introduce the retail analytics problem and explain that the data is synthetic.
2. Open Overview and walk through the headline KPIs and revenue trends.
3. Use Sales Explorer filters to compare regions/categories and describe one observation.
4. Open Data Quality Lab and explain duplicates, missing cells, invalid rows, and the IQR policy.
5. Open Customer Segments and explain RFM features, scaling, K-Means, and silhouette diagnostics.
6. Close with limitations: demo data, assumed profit fallback, and the need to validate business definitions.
